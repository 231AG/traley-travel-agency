## Phase 5: Design B ("Departures")

**Built.** Every page mirrored under `/b/`, sharing content, fields, validation, the WhatsApp builder and the controller with Design A. Concept: the site reads like the departures board at Roberts International. Big Shoulders Display (signage) for headings and the board, Atkinson Hyperlegible for body text. Mostly white pages with ruled rows instead of cards; navy used only for the board system and footer; gold for board text and primary buttons. The hero is a large headline plus the board; each board row has real "Flights" and "Visa" buttons that open the quote form below already filled in, and the arriving row opens the concierge form. Rows turn over once on load (skipped under reduced motion). The quote form is a plain "check-in counter" panel with a vertical tab column on desktop. Photos appear only as wide strips. Inner pages carry a one-row board strip (the destination's own row on visa pages, the arriving row on concierge). All B pages are `noindex` with canonical URLs pointing at Design A; `npm run check:remove-b` proves deleting `src/designs/b`, `src/pages/b` and `src/styles/b.css` leaves a working site.
