## 2. What was built, and how to run and deploy it

- **Stack**: Astro 7 static output, TypeScript (strictest), Tailwind CSS 4 with brand tokens only, self-hosted subset fonts, `zod/mini`, `sharp` image pipeline, Playwright + axe-core, Lighthouse, Vitest, ESLint, Prettier. Astro replaced Next.js because an empty Next.js 16 static page already ships about 133 KB of gzipped JS against the 100 KB budget (decision D1 in `PLAN.md`); this site ships at most 11 KB.
- **Pages** (both designs): home, flights & hotels, visa assistance, six visa destination pages (US, UK, Canada, Schengen Area, UAE, China), Liberia concierge, about, contact, 404.
- **Quote flow**: Flights / Visa / Concierge tabs; validation; a live boarding-pass stub (Design A); every service, destination and board button opens the right tab already filled in; `?quote=visa&to=Canada` deep links; the message opens in WhatsApp (`wa.me/231886504519`) with empty fields left out. It handles blocked popups, double clicks and edits after sending.
- **Content**: everything in `src/content/site.ts`. `PLACEHOLDERS.md` and `CREDITS.md` are generated from it.
- **Run**: `npm install`, `npm run dev`. **Check**: `npm run check`. **Deploy**: Cloudflare Pages, build command `npm run build`, output `dist`, `NODE_VERSION=22`, custom domain `www.tarleytravel.com`. Details are in `README.md`.

3. **Email domain.** `info@tarleytravelllc.com` is on a different domain from the website (`tarleytravel.com`). Confirm it receives mail, or set up `info@tarleytravel.com`.
4. **Photos.** Real photos of the team and the office front should replace the two placeholders. Tarley's own photos of trips or the office would also be better than the two CC0 stock photos.
5. **Facts to confirm.** Service promises and visa types listed in `PLACEHOLDERS.md` need a yes from Tarley before launch.
6. **Links to recheck.** `npm run check:links` cannot reach the US State Department or Instagram from a server (they refuse automated requests). Both were correct when built; recheck them by hand every few months.
7. **Code.** `src/shared/quote/controller.ts` is 422 lines (a module, not a component). Splitting out the floating-button and orientation helpers would make it easier to maintain.
8. **Photo strip (Design B).** It downloads a 3:2 file for a wide crop (lazy-loaded, under 75 KB). Strip-specific crops would save about half of that.
9. **Repository size.** The handover screenshots are about 58 MB of PNGs in `screenshots/`. After the owner has reviewed them, they can move to a release attachment or Git LFS.
10. **Unsplash connector.** It needs the Unsplash account's email address confirmed before it can be used for future photos.

Next steps: fill the placeholders, choose a design, add the Cloudflare Pages project and domain, and turn on analytics with a Cloudflare Web Analytics token if wanted.

## 7. Recommendation: Design A or Design B

**I recommend Design A for launch.** The owner decides; both designs are complete and pass every budget.

- **Trust.** A is the design Tarley already approved. It leads with a real photo of Roberts International and the calm Playfair and Poppins type of an established agency. B's signage type and board feel bold and current, but a first-time visitor sending passport details may read A as the steadier business.
- **Conversion.** A puts the quote form in the first scroll on a phone (the card starts 640 px down at 390 px wide), so the 60-second quote target is easy. On a phone, B's board comes first and the form starts about two screens down (1,740 px at 390 px wide). B's strength is that one tap on a destination opens a prefilled form, and A already borrows that idea: its destination cards and service buttons do the same.
- **Performance.** Both are effectively equal: under 11 KB of JS, LCP about 2 s on throttled mobile, CLS near zero. B loads about 2 KB more font.
- **What I would carry over from B.** The "From Monrovia (ROB): where we help you go" board would work as a section further down A's homepage, or on A's visa page, if Tarley likes it.
